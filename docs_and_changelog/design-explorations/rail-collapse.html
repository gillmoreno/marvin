Five ways to collapse the sidebar

Same Marvin rail in each. Click around. Nothing here is wired to the app — pick the one that feels right, or name the mix.

1 · Bottom control What you have now. “Collapse” sits under your name. The rail shrinks to icons.
2 · Seam tab A chevron on the join between rail and page. The control lives on the edge, not in the menu.
3 · Hide completely The rail leaves. A thin dark edge, or “Show sidebar” in the page, brings it back.
4 · Hover to open Stays as icons. Hover expands it. Pin keeps it open so it stops collapsing when you leave.
5 · Overlay drawer The page is always full width. A menu button slides the rail over the work, then it goes away.
